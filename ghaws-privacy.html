Al-Ghaws: Pearl Diver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · This policy covers the app for iPhone and iPad.

The short version

Al-Ghaws collects no data at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchase, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. Gameplay runs entirely on your device, and the app works fully offline.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your chosen language, whether sound is on, and the stars you have earned on each dive. All of it stays on your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not collect

No accounts, no email address, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone or camera access, no identifiers and no personal information of any kind. The app makes no network request. The only link it shows is to this policy.

Children

Al-Ghaws is made for children and families. It is an Education app rated 4+ on the App Store. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
