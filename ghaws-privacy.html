Al-Ghaws: Pearl Diver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

The short version

Al-Ghaws collects no data at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDK.

What stays on your device

The app stores four things: your chosen language, whether sound is on, the stars you have earned on each dive, and whether the optional unlock has been purchased. All of it is kept in the app's own storage on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

Network use

Gameplay is entirely offline — the game works in airplane mode and makes no network requests. The single exception is that if you choose to buy or restore the optional in-app purchase, your device talks to Apple's App Store (or Google Play on Android) to complete it. We never see your payment details; that transaction is handled by the store under its own policy.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and designed for children. It contains no advertising, no external links, no user accounts and no data collection. The optional purchase sits behind a parental gate — a multiplication question — so a child cannot reach the payment sheet unaided.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
