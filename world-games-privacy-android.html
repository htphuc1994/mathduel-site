Math Duel Players: World Games for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · This policy covers the Android app from Google Play.

The short version

World Games itself collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no in-app purchase. All of its games run entirely on your device. The app is supported by advertising from Google AdMob: a banner on the list of games and on each game's start screen, and now and then a full-screen advertisement between two different games, both described in full below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner advertisement, in its own strip at the bottom of the screen, and only on the list of games and on each game's start screen (including beneath the language and how-to-play sheets that open from them). It is never shown while a game is being played, nor on a game's result screen. It appears only after a short pause and only when no finger is touching the screen, so a tap meant for a game does not land on it.

A full-screen advertisement may also appear when a game is chosen from the list of games, but only when it is a different game from the one opened last. It never appears in the first five minutes after the app starts, nor within five minutes of the previous one, nor when the same game is opened again, and never while a game is being played. It too waits until no finger is touching the screen, the banner is hidden while it is shown, and the chosen game opens as soon as it is closed.

  • Non-personalised. Every ad request, for the banner and for the full-screen advertisement alike, is made as a non-personalised request, so ads are chosen from the general context, not from a profile of the player.
  • Child-directed. Because the games are made for children, every request is tagged for child-directed treatment, and Google's consent request is tagged for users under the age of consent.
  • General audiences only. Ad content is limited to Google's "General" maximum content rating.
  • The advertising ID is never read. The app is built without permission to read the device's advertising ID, and without Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions.
  • Not used to track you. We do not link any advertising data to a person, and we receive no report that identifies anyone.

To deliver an ad and keep it free of fraud, Google's advertising software in the app receives the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate a general region), whether an ad was shown and tapped, information about how the software itself is performing, and an identifier for the app on that device (the app set ID). See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent

Because World Games is made for children, every request it makes to Google's consent tool says that the player is under the age of consent. The tool therefore does not ask anyone for consent to personalised advertising, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom or anywhere else, and none is used: only non-personalised, child-directed ads are requested.

The app still asks Google's consent tool first, every time it starts, and does not start the advertising software until that tool allows it. If the tool ever reports that a choice is needed, a Privacy and cookie settings button appears at the bottom of the list of games, so that the choice can be made or changed at any time. Withdrawing it removes the advertising.

Offline

The games need no network. With no connection the app simply shows no advertisement; it raises no error and every game remains fully playable.

What stays on your device

The app remembers the language you choose (shared by all of its games), whether its short guide to the map has been shown, and, for each game, things such as whether sound is on, the mode and level you last picked, the stars or puzzles you have completed, and whether you have already seen the rules; Google's consent component also stores your consent choice. All of it stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app. The app does not back this data up.

What the app does not collect

No accounts, no email address, no contacts, no access to the device's location, no photos, no microphone or camera access, and no personal information of any kind. (Google's estimate of a general region from the IP address is described under Advertising above.) The only link the app itself shows is to this policy, and it opens only after a grown-up answers a question.

Children

World Games is made for children aged about 6 to 12 and their families, and on Google Play it follows the Families policy. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the advertising is configured as described above.
