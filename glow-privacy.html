Glow — Privacy Policy

Beautiful selfies, edited privately on your device.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

The short version: Glow edits photos locally on your device. Glow’s developer does not receive your photos, face data, or other personal information. Glow has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Photo and face processing does not use a network connection. Apple services and destinations that you explicitly choose, such as StoreKit, iCloud Photos, or an app selected from the system share sheet, operate under their own settings and privacy policies.

What Glow collects

Glow’s developer collects no data from the app. We operate no account system or backend that receives user photos, face data, editing activity, device identifiers, or subscription details. Glow includes no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or tracking SDKs.

Photos, camera & exports

  • A photo you select or take is decoded into a temporary working copy and processed locally on your device. Glow does not upload it to us or to an AI, analytics, or advertising service.
  • Your original image remains in the location from which you selected it. Apple Photos and iCloud Photos may retrieve or synchronize that image according to your device and Apple account settings; Glow does not control those services.
  • When you tap Export, Glow passes the final edited image to the iOS system share sheet. You decide whether to save it to Photos or send it to another app or service. A destination you select may store or upload the image under its own privacy policy. Glow does not automatically select a destination or receive a copy.
  • The exported image contains the final image pixels only. Glow does not attach its temporary face rectangles or landmark coordinates to the exported file.

Face data and on-device processing

Glow does not collect or transmit face data off your device. When a selected photo contains a face, Apple’s on-device Vision framework may temporarily create a face observation. Glow reads only face bounding rectangles and approximate positions of the left eye, right eye, and nose. These positions are used only to anchor face effects and reshape controls and to help distinguish the main subject from other people in the Clean People feature.

Portrait smoothing uses local pixel and color processing. If you enable Enhance, a fixed Core ML model bundled inside the app may process the pixels of the entire edited image, including any face visible in it. Apple Vision, Core ML, and the bundled model all execute on the device. Glow does not create faceprints, embeddings, biometric templates, recognize or identify anyone, compare faces, authenticate users, or infer identity or sensitive characteristics. User photos and face data are never used to train or improve a model.

Storage, retention & deletion

Glow’s app code does not write the working photo, Vision face observations, face coordinates, masks, or edit anchors to app files, persistent browser storage, logs, a developer server, or a cloud database. They may remain in volatile app memory while the Glow process is running, including while the app is temporarily suspended. When another photo is loaded, the previous working state is replaced and becomes eligible for memory release. All remaining working data is released when the app process terminates.

Because we never receive this data, we have no server copy to retain or delete. You control the original photo and any exported image and can delete them from Photos or from the destination you selected. Force-quitting Glow terminates its current in-memory editing process.

Third-party AI, sharing & network use

Glow does not use an external or remote third-party AI provider or API. It does not send photos, face data, or other personal information to an AI provider. Image processing uses Apple Vision, Apple Core ML, local WebGL processing, and a fixed model bundled in the app. All processing executes on the device, and no AI provider receives the input or result.

Photo and face processing makes no network requests. StoreKit may contact Apple to load products, verify entitlements, complete purchases, restore purchases, and manage subscriptions. Privacy and Terms links open in your browser. Apple Photos, iCloud, and a share-sheet destination may communicate over the network only according to your settings or explicit destination choice. None of these flows sends face rectangles or landmark coordinates to Glow’s developer.

Subscriptions & payments

Glow offers an optional auto-renewable subscription. Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit under your Apple ID. Glow may receive product availability and an on-device entitlement status, but we do not receive or store your Apple ID or payment details, and no subscription information is sent to a developer server.

Information stored on your device

Glow persistently stores only small local preferences: your chosen language and a cached Boolean indicating whether Premium was active. These values contain no photo or face data and are not sent to us.

Children

Glow is a general-audience app and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
