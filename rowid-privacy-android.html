Rowid for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · This policy covers the Android app from Google Play.

The short version

Rowid itself collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics of our own, and the files you open never leave your device.

Rowid is free, and it is supported by advertising from Google AdMob, described in full below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rowid shows two kinds of advertisement:

  • A banner at the bottom of the first screen and of the screen that shows a file. It sits below your data and never covers it.
  • Sometimes, a full-screen advertisement when you leave a file and return to the first screen. It is never shown in the first five minutes after the app starts, never more than once in five minutes, never when the app opens, and never while a file is open.

To show an advertisement, measure it and keep it free of fraud, Google's advertising software in the app receives the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate a general region), the device's advertising ID, an identifier for the app on that device (the app set ID), whether an advertisement was shown or tapped, and information about how the software itself is performing. Google handles this under Google's Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We receive only totals from AdMob, never anything that identifies you.

Personalised advertising and your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Rowid asks Google's consent tool first, every time it starts, and requests no advertisement until that tool allows it. If your consent is needed, the tool asks for it, and advertisements are personalised only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time under About → Privacy choices.

Elsewhere, Google may personalise advertisements using the device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android's settings (on most devices under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads). On devices that support Android's Privacy Sandbox, Google's advertising software may also use its advertising features, such as ad topics, which can be turned off in the same place. You can also review how Google personalises advertisements in My Ad Center.

Your files stay on your device

Rowid is a read-only viewer. When you open a file, it makes a private copy inside its own storage and reads that copy, never your original, so it cannot alter your data. While the file is open, Rowid re-checks your original's size and modification date and shows whether it is unchanged.

The private copy is deleted when you close the file, and anything left behind by an interrupted session is deleted the next time the app starts. Export creates a file only when you ask for it and hands it to Android's share sheet, so it goes only where you choose to send it.

What stays on your device

  • Recent files: the name, size, format and time you opened each one, plus Android's permission to reopen it with a tap. That permission is a handle for a file you already chose, not the file's contents.
  • Saved queries: any SQL you choose to name and keep.
  • Your language, if you chose one in About.
  • Your consent choice, kept by Google's consent tool.

All of it stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app. The app does not back it up. You can clear the recent list at any time with Clear on the first screen.

Network use

Rowid's own code makes no network requests: opening, browsing, searching, sorting, querying and exporting a file never contacts anything. The only network traffic comes from Google's advertising software and its consent tool. Without a connection Rowid works fully and simply shows no advertisement.

What the app does not collect

No account, no email address, no contacts, no photos, and no camera or microphone access. Rowid asks for no location permission, so neither the app nor the advertising software in it can read the device's location. (Google's estimate of a general region from the IP address is described under Advertising above.) Your files and their contents are never uploaded, indexed or seen by anyone but you.

Children

Rowid is a developer tool for adults. On Google Play it is offered for ages 18 and over, and it is not directed at children.

Your rights

Rowid keeps no personal data off your device, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete. For advertising, use the choices described above.
