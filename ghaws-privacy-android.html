Al-Ghaws: Pearl Diver for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · This policy covers the Android app from Google Play.

The short version

Al-Ghaws itself collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no in-app purchase. Gameplay runs entirely on your device.

The app is supported by a single banner advertisement from Google AdMob, described in full below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner advertisement, and only on the start screen (including beneath the how-to-play and language panels, which open from it). It is never shown while a dive is being played, nor on the panels that end a dive: the dive controls sit at the bottom of the screen, where the banner would be, so it never appears under a finger that is still playing.

  • Non-personalised. Every ad request is made as a non-personalised request, so ads are chosen from the general context, not from a profile of the player.
  • Child-directed. Because the game is made for children, every request is tagged for child-directed treatment, and Google's consent request is tagged for users under the age of consent.
  • General audiences only. Ad content is limited to Google's "General" maximum content rating.
  • The advertising ID is never read. The app is built without permission to read the device's advertising ID, and without Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions.
  • Not used to track you. We do not link any advertising data to a person, and we receive no report that identifies anyone.

To deliver an ad and keep it free of fraud, Google's advertising software in the app receives the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate a general region), whether an ad was shown and tapped, information about how the software itself is performing, and an identifier for the app on that device (the app set ID). See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent

Because Al-Ghaws is made for children, every request it makes to Google's consent tool says that the player is under the age of consent. The tool therefore does not ask anyone for consent to personalised advertising, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom or anywhere else, and none is used: only non-personalised, child-directed ads are requested.

The app still asks Google's consent tool first, every time it starts, and does not start the advertising SDK until that tool allows it. If the tool ever reports that a choice is needed, the app asks for it before any ad is requested, and Privacy and cookie settings appears on the start screen so that the choice can be changed at any time.

Offline

Gameplay needs no network. With no connection the app simply shows no advertisement; it raises no error and remains fully playable.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your chosen language, whether sound is on, and the stars you have earned on each dive; Google's consent component also stores your consent choice. All of it stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app. The app does not back this data up.

What the app does not collect

No accounts, no email address, no contacts, no access to the device's location, no photos, no microphone or camera access, and no personal information of any kind. (Google's estimate of a general region from the IP address is described under Advertising above.) The only link the app itself shows is to this policy.

Children

Al-Ghaws is made for children and families, and on Google Play it follows the Families policy. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the advertising is configured as described above.
