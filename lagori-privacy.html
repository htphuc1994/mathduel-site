Lagori: Seven Stones — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The short version

Lagori itself collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no in-app purchase. Gameplay runs entirely on your device.

The Android app is supported by a single banner advertisement from Google AdMob, described in full below. The iOS app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics SDK.

Advertising on Android (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows one banner advertisement. It appears on the start screen and beneath the result, pass-the-ball, how-to-play and language panels. It is never shown while a round is being played: the aim and stacking controls sit at the bottom of the screen, so the banner is hidden for the whole of a throw and a rebuild.

  • Non-personalised. Every ad request is made as a non-personalised request, so ads are chosen from the general context, not from a profile of the player.
  • Child-directed. Because the game is made for children, every request is tagged for child-directed treatment, and Google's consent request is tagged for users under the age of consent.
  • General audiences only. Ad content is limited to Google's "General" maximum content rating.
  • Not used to track you. We do not link any advertising data to a person, and we receive no report that identifies anyone.

To deliver an ad, Google receives technical information such as the type of device, a coarse region and the fact that an ad slot was shown. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choice

Where consent is required, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form asks before any ad is requested, and the advertising SDK is not started until your choice allows it. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy and cookie settings on the app's start screen.

Offline

Gameplay needs no network. With no connection the Android app simply shows no advertisement; it raises no error and remains fully playable.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your chosen language, whether sound is on, and the stars you have earned in each yard. On Android, Google's consent component also stores your consent choice. All of it stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app; the Android app does not back this data up.

What the app does not collect

No accounts, no email address, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone or camera access, and no personal information of any kind. The only link the app itself shows is to this policy.

Children

Lagori is made for children and families. On Google Play it follows the Families policy; on the App Store it is an Education app rated 4+ and is not in the Kids Category. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and all advertising on Android is configured as described above.
