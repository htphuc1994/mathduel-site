CAD Viewer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

The short version

CAD Viewer collects no data at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK of any kind. The models you open are never uploaded and never seen by anyone but you. The app asks for no permissions — not the camera, not photos, not location, not contacts, not notifications — because it needs none.

Your files stay on your device

CAD Viewer is a read-only viewer. When you open a model it reads your original file where it already is, and only while you are looking at it. Every format — STEP, IGES, IFC, DXF, glTF, USD, STL, point clouds, G-code and the rest — is parsed and drawn on your device by code that ships inside the app.

Your original file is never written to, renamed, moved or deleted. The app asks iOS for permission to read the file you chose, and hands that permission back when the file has loaded.

It reads the one file you picked, and does not go looking through the folder around it. Once a file is open, the app's parsers are working on that file's bytes and have no way to reach another. A glTF file that points at a separate .bin on the internet is refused with an explanation rather than fetched.

One thing worth knowing is iOS's doing rather than ours: when another app sends you a file it cannot share in place, iOS itself puts a copy in CAD Viewer's Inbox folder and hands the app that. CAD Viewer neither creates nor deletes those; they go when you delete the app.

When you save a copy

Tapping Save a copy in the app is the only thing that makes CAD Viewer copy your file, and it only ever copies it into the app's own storage on the same device. A saved copy:

  • stays until you delete it — closing the model, quitting the app and updating the app all leave it alone;
  • keeps its original filename, and never overwrites an existing one (a second part.stp becomes part (2).stp);
  • can be deleted from inside the app, one at a time or all at once, which deletes the real file;
  • is visible in the Files app under CAD Viewer, and over a USB connection to a computer. That is deliberate — it is how you get a file back out — but it does mean anyone with your unlocked device, or a computer you have trusted, can read it.

What else stays on your device

CAD Viewer keeps a few small things in its own storage, all local to your device:

  • Recent files — for up to twelve files: the filename, the date you opened it, how many triangles, line segments or points it held, and a bookmark so a tap can reopen it. A bookmark is a handle iOS issues for a file you already chose; it records where the file is, not what is in it. Clear on the first screen removes the list and its bookmarks.
  • Your viewer preferences — the default background, and whether auto-rotate starts on.
  • Your chosen language, if you picked one instead of following the device.
  • A count of how many files you have opened today, and the day it refers to. Two integers: no filenames, no paths, no history.

The app writes no flag of its own about whether you have paid — it asks the App Store's on-device record each time. Deleting the app removes everything above, together with any copies you saved. (iOS's Offload App is different: it removes the app and deliberately keeps its documents and data.)

Backups

Saved copies live in the app's Documents folder and the settings above live in its preferences, so both are included in your device's backup if you use one — exactly as your other apps' documents are. CAD Viewer does not exclude them, and it never uploads anything itself: a backup is made by your device, not by this app.

With iCloud Backup on, those copies are stored in your iCloud account. With a Finder or iTunes backup they are stored on your computer, and are only encrypted there if you tick Encrypt local backup. You can stop this app being backed up at all in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups.

Network use

CAD Viewer contains no networking code of its own — no URL loading, no sockets, no web view. Opening and reading a model contacts nothing, and the app works fully in airplane mode.

There is one exception, and it is Apple's rather than ours: the App Store's in-app purchase system (StoreKit). To be exact about when it happens —

  • at launch, the app asks for the current price of the unlock in your local currency, so the purchase screen can show a real price instead of a guess. This happens whether or not you ever open that screen, and it fails quietly with no connection;
  • when you buy, or tap Restore Purchase;
  • in the background, the app keeps a listener open so a purchase made on another device, or a refund, reaches it without waiting for the next launch.

We never see your payment details, your Apple Account, or any identifier tied to you. Those transactions are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; the app reads only which product was bought and whether it was refunded. Nothing about your files is ever sent anywhere, by this app or by anything in it.

One more piece of iOS behaviour to be straight about: if the file you open is stored in iCloud Drive or another cloud provider rather than on the device, the system downloads it before the app can read it. That is the provider's transfer, not ours, and it is the same thing that happens when you open the file in any other app.

Purchases

CAD Viewer is free to use, and every feature is free: opening, viewing, measuring, section views, saving copies, all eleven languages and every supported format. What the free tier limits is how many files you may open in a day — one. The count resets at midnight, local time. The sample models bundled with the app never count against it, and a file the app fails to read does not count either.

CAD Viewer Pro removes that limit. It is a single one-time purchase, not a subscription: nothing renews, there is nothing to cancel, and payment is charged once to your Apple Account at confirmation. If you reinstall or change device, Restore Purchase brings it back at no cost.

Whether you have paid is verified on your device, against a signed record the App Store keeps there — which is why the app stays unlocked with no signal, and why a refund re-locks it.

Children

CAD Viewer is a tool for people who work with CAD files and is not directed at children, though it is rated 4+ because there is nothing in it that would not be. It contains no advertising, no user accounts, no user-generated content, no chat, no links out of the app and no data collection. The optional purchase is handled entirely by the App Store, which applies its own parental controls.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete — we have nothing of yours. Everything the app keeps is on your device, under your control, and is removed when you delete it.
