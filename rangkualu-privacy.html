Privacy Policy — Rangku Alu: Count the Beat 🎍

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Rangku Alu: Count the Beat does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It is a solo, two-player (same-device), and vs-computer educational maths game — bamboo poles open and shut on a repeating beat, and you work out which beats they stay open — that runs entirely offline on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. It does not request your name, email, contacts, location, photos, identifiers, or any other personal data.

In-app purchase

The game is free and offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase (an unlock for the later solo worlds), placed behind a parental gate. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play using your existing store account; the app itself receives and stores no payment or personal information — only an on-device note that the unlock is owned, so it keeps working offline. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no consumable purchases.

Network & third parties

Gameplay makes no network connections and the app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, trackers, or external links. All fonts, artwork and sounds are bundled — the audio is generated by the app itself rather than downloaded — so the game works fully offline (for example, in airplane mode). The only network use is the optional purchase/restore described above, handled by the platform store when you choose to use it.

Data storage

Game state (the current puzzle, which beats you picked, each player's score, whose turn it is, your chosen language and sound setting) exists only in memory while you play and is discarded when the app closes. Two small on-device notes persist: a flag recording that the optional unlock was purchased, and a count of the stars earned in each world so solo practice remembers your progress. Neither contains personal information and neither ever leaves your device.

Children's privacy

Rangku Alu is suitable for children. Because it collects no data and contains no ads, external links, or communication features — and any purchase requires passing a parental gate — it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
