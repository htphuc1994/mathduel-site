Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock the full game?

On the start screen, tap Unlock Full Weave (or tap a locked Medium/Hard difficulty in Solo mode). A short one-time parental check appears, then the App Store purchase sheet.

I already bought the unlock — how do I get it on a new device?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and restore for free.

The app works with no internet — is that normal?

Yes. Our games are fully offline by design. The internet is used only for the optional purchase or restore, which the App Store handles.

Do you collect any data?

No. We collect no personal data, have no accounts, and show no ads. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I change the language?

Tap the 🌐 globe button on the start screen and pick from the list — our games support 11 languages.

I’m having another problem.

Please email htphuc.cs@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version, and we’ll help.
