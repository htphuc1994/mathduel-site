Ring the Pots — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · This policy covers the iPhone app from the App Store and the game in a web browser.

The short version

Ring the Pots collects no data at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no purchases and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDK.

What stays on your device

The app remembers four things: your chosen language, whether sound is on, the mode, opponent and stall you last picked, and whether you have already seen the rules. All of it is kept in the app's own storage on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

Network use

None. The game is a single offline page and makes no network requests at all; it works the same in airplane mode. The only link it shows is to this policy, on the start screen, and it opens in your browser.

Children

The app is made for children aged about 7 to 11 and their families. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user accounts and no data collection, and its only link is to this policy.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

鶯歌套圈圈 — 隱私權政策（摘要）

本遊戲不收集任何資料。沒有帳號、沒有登入、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告、沒有內購，也不追蹤使用者。 遊戲只在你的裝置上記住：語言、音效開關、上次選的玩法／對手／攤位，以及是否看過規則說明；這些資料不會傳送到任何地方，刪除 App 時一併刪除。 遊戲完全離線，不會發出任何網路連線；開始畫面上唯一的連結就是這份隱私權政策。
