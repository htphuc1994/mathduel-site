Privacy Policy — Abacus: Bead Math 🧮

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Abacus: Bead Math itself does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It is a single-player educational counting toy — a virtual abacus in four historical constructions — whose gameplay runs entirely offline on your device. The app is free, has no in-app purchases, and is supported by advertising, which is described in full below.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of our own. It does not request your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The iOS app shows a single banner advertisement, supplied by Google AdMob. It appears on the start screen, the win screen, and beneath the pause, how-to-play and language panels, and is hidden for the whole of a round. The abacus fills the screen down to the bottom edge and every bead is tappable across its full rod column and stacking pitch, so there is never an advertisement on screen while a problem is being solved.

  • Non-personalised. Every request is made as a non-personalised ad request, so ads are based on the general context rather than any profile of the player.
  • Child-directed. Because the audience is children, every request is tagged for child-directed treatment and for users under the age of consent. No advertising identifier is transmitted, and the app never presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you.
  • General audiences only. Ad content is restricted to Google's "General" maximum content rating.
  • Not used for cross-app tracking. We do not link any advertising data to a person, and we receive no report identifying any individual.

To deliver an ad, Google receives technical information such as a device type, a coarse region and the fact that an ad slot was shown. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choice. Where consent is required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google's User Messaging Platform asks before any ad is requested, and no advertising SDK is started until that choice allows it. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy and cookie settings link on the app's start screen.

Offline. With no network connection the app simply shows no advertisement. It raises no error and remains completely playable.

What the app does not collect

No accounts, no sign-up, no email address, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone or camera access, no personal information of any kind, and no external links other than this policy and the privacy controls described above.

In-app purchases

There are none. Earlier versions of Abacus: Bead Math offered a one-time purchase that unlocked the harder solo levels. That purchase has been removed and every mode and difficulty is now free for everyone, including anyone who never bought it.

Data storage

Game state (the bead positions, the current problem, your chosen language and settings) exists only in memory while you play and is discarded when the app closes. Google's advertising and consent components store their own settings on the device, including your recorded consent choice.

Children's privacy

Abacus: Bead Math is an Education app rated 4+ and is suitable for children. It is not published in Apple's Kids Category and is not a "Made for Kids" app. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and all advertising is configured as child-directed, non-personalised and limited to general-audience content, as described above. Parents who prefer to limit advertising further can do so in the device's own settings.
