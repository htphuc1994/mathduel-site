Falaj: Share the Water — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app from the App Store and the game in a web browser.

The short version

Falaj collects no data at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no purchases and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDK.

What stays on your device

The app remembers one thing: your chosen language. It is kept in the app's own storage on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

Network use

None. Gameplay is entirely offline: the game makes no network requests and works the same in airplane mode. Apart from a link to this policy, which opens in your browser, the app shows no links.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is made for children and their families. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user accounts and no data collection. Every channel and both farmers are included; there is nothing to buy or unlock.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: htphuc.cs@gmail.com

Falaj: Share the Water is inspired by the aflaj irrigation systems of Oman, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, in which a village's water is shared by time rather than by volume. The game divides the day into 24 shares for the sake of clean fractions; traditional athar shares were about half an hour and the divisions varied from one falaj to another.
