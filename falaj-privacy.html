Falaj: Share the Water — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

The short version

Falaj collects no data at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDK.

What stays on your device

The app stores two things: your chosen language, and whether the optional unlock has been purchased. Both are kept in the app's own storage on your device, are never transmitted anywhere, and are removed when you delete the app.

Network use

Gameplay is entirely offline. The game works in airplane mode and makes no network requests — there is no networking code in it. The single exception is that if you choose to buy or restore the optional in-app purchase, your device talks to Apple's App Store (or Google Play on Android) to complete it. We never see your payment details; that transaction is handled by the store under its own policy.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is designed for children. It contains no advertising, no external links, no user accounts and no data collection. The optional purchase is handled entirely by the App Store, which applies its own parental controls.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: htphuc.cs@gmail.com

Falaj: Share the Water is inspired by the aflaj irrigation systems of Oman, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, in which a village's water is shared by time rather than by volume. The game divides the day into 24 shares for the sake of clean fractions; traditional athar shares were about half an hour and the divisions varied from one falaj to another.
