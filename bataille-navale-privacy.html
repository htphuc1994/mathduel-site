Privacy Policy — Bataille Navale: Sea Duel ⚓️

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Bataille Navale: Sea Duel is a two-player (same-device) and solo coordinate-grid battleship game. The game runs entirely on your device and is playable offline. The app itself does not collect, store, or share any personal information — it has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of its own. However, the app is free and supported by advertising, which involves a third-party ad provider as described below.

Information the app collects

None directly. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal data. Your only settings (chosen language and sound on/off) are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, we display ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain data, such as device identifiers and usage/diagnostic information. We have configured the ads to be:

  • Non-personalized — ads are not based on a profile of your interests.
  • Rated for general audiences (G) — suitable for all ages.
  • Not used for cross-app tracking — the app does not present the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Google's collection and use of this data are governed by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data / advertising, and how partners use information. When the device is offline, no ads load and no ad data is exchanged — the game remains fully playable.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, logins, names, or email addresses.
  • No precise location. (Google may infer only a coarse region to keep ads relevant/appropriate.)
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No first-party analytics, tracking, or profiles built by us.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children & families

The game is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. It is listed under Education and is not part of Apple's "Made for Kids" category. Ads are non-personalized and limited to a general-audience (G) rating, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians can further limit ad-related data through their device's privacy settings.

Your choices

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and Tracking, to control advertising options.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, to reset or delete your advertising ID.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
