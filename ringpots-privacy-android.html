Ring the Pots for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · This policy covers the Android app from Google Play.

The short version

Ring the Pots itself collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no in-app purchase. Gameplay runs entirely on your device.

The app is supported by a single banner advertisement from Google AdMob, described in full below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner advertisement, and only on the start screen (including beneath the language and how-to-play sheets, which open from it). It is never shown while a game is being played, nor on the result screen that follows the last throw: the ring bag and the Throw button sit at the bottom of the screen, where the banner would be, so it never appears under a finger that is still playing.

  • Non-personalised. Every ad request is made as a non-personalised request, so ads are chosen from the general context, not from a profile of the player.
  • Child-directed. Because the game is made for children, every request is tagged for child-directed treatment, and Google's consent request is tagged for users under the age of consent.
  • General audiences only. Ad content is limited to Google's "General" maximum content rating.
  • The advertising ID is never read. The app is built without permission to read the device's advertising ID, and without Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions.
  • Not used to track you. We do not link any advertising data to a person, and we receive no report that identifies anyone.

To deliver an ad and keep it free of fraud, Google's advertising software in the app receives the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate a general region), whether an ad was shown and tapped, information about how the software itself is performing, and an identifier for the app on that device (the app set ID). See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent

Because Ring the Pots is made for children, every request it makes to Google's consent tool says that the player is under the age of consent. The tool therefore does not ask anyone for consent to personalised advertising, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom or anywhere else, and none is used: only non-personalised, child-directed ads are requested.

The app still asks Google's consent tool first, every time it starts, and does not start the advertising software until that tool allows it. If the tool ever reports that a choice is needed, the app asks for it before any ad is requested, and a Privacy settings button appears in the how-to-play sheet (the ? button on the start screen), under the privacy paragraph, so that the choice can be changed at any time. Withdrawing it removes the banner.

Offline

Gameplay needs no network. With no connection the app simply shows no advertisement; it raises no error and remains fully playable.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your chosen language, whether sound is on, the mode, opponent and stall you last picked, and whether you have already seen the rules; Google's consent component also stores your consent choice. All of it stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app. The app does not back this data up.

What the app does not collect

No accounts, no email address, no contacts, no access to the device's location, no photos, no microphone or camera access, and no personal information of any kind. (Google's estimate of a general region from the IP address is described under Advertising above.) The only link the app itself shows is to this policy.

Children

Ring the Pots is made for children aged about 7 to 11 and their families, and on Google Play it follows the Families policy. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the advertising is configured as described above.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

鶯歌套圈圈（Android 版）— 隱私權政策（摘要）

遊戲本身不收集任何個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有登入、沒有我們自己的分析工具，也沒有內購。 Android 版只在開始畫面上顯示一個來自 Google AdMob 的小橫幅廣告，遊戲進行中與結算畫面都不會出現。 廣告一律以「非個人化」、「兒童導向」的方式請求，內容限「普遍級」，App 也不具備讀取裝置廣告 ID 的權限。 顯示廣告時，Google 會收到一般廣告請求都會帶的資訊，例如裝置的 IP 位址（可能用來推估大致地區）、廣告是否顯示或被點擊、軟體效能資訊，以及這個 App 在裝置上的識別碼（app set ID）。 遊戲只在你的裝置上記住語言、音效開關、上次的選擇，以及是否看過規則說明；Google 的同意元件也會記住你的同意選擇。 離線時遊戲照常能玩，只是不會出現廣告。
